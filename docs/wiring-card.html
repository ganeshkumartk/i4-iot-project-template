Jumper Checklist

  • USB → ESP32 (power)
  • 3V3 → Moisture VCC
  • D34 → Moisture AO
  • D26 → LL LV1
  • 3V3 rail → LL LV
  • 5V rail → LL HV + Relay VCC
  • LL HV1 → Relay IN
  • Top GND ↔ Bottom GND
  • COM → 5V, NO → Pump+
  • Pump (−) → GND
